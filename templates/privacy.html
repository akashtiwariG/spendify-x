Privacy Policy

Data We Collect

We collect financial data you choose to store in Spendly, including transaction amounts, descriptions, categories, dates, and payment methods. We also collect account information such as your email address, username, and hashed password for authentication purposes.

How We Use Your Data

Your financial data is used solely to provide expense tracking services, generate spending reports, and help you manage your personal finances. We do not use your data for advertising, marketing, or any commercial purposes beyond providing the core functionality of Spendly.

Data Storage

All your financial data is stored securely in our database. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. Your data remains private and is not shared with third parties.

Third Party Services

We use third-party services for hosting, database management, and authentication. These service providers have access to your data only as necessary to perform their services and are obligated to maintain the confidentiality of your information. We do not sell your data to any third parties.

Data Retention

We retain your financial data as long as your account remains active. If you delete your account, we will permanently delete all your financial data from our systems within 30 days, subject to any legal obligations to retain certain data.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, modify, or delete your financial data at any time. You can export your data from the application or request deletion of your account through account settings.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will notify you by updating the "Last Updated" date at the bottom of this page. Your continued use of Spendly after such changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated privacy policy.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or our data practices, please contact us at privacy@spendly.app.
